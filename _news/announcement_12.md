Some news: I’ve started as a postdoc in the Computational Systems Biology group at the Universität Salzburg, working with Prof. Nikolaus Fortelny.

I’ll be working on EpiFlaMe, a consortium of seven research groups studying inflammatory memory in epithelial cells: how past inflammation leaves lasting molecular changes that shape organ-specific responses and cancer risk. I’m really excited about this one: it’s a great question, and a great mix of people to work with.

Looking forward to what lies ahead, and to learning a lot from this new project!
